Why Use This Portal?

We are committed to providing a transparent, fair, and quick resolution process for every campus grievance.

100% Confidential

Your issues are handled with utmost privacy and routed directly to the authorized department heads.

Quick Action

Dedicated grievance committee ensures all submissions are reviewed within 24 to 48 working hours.

Transparent Process

Track the progress of your submitted complaints in real-time with reference IDs.

How It Works

Simple 3-step process to get your issues resolved

01

Submit Details

Fill out the online grievance form with complete information regarding your issue.

02

Review & Route

The grievance cell reviews and assigns your ticket to the respective Dean or Officer.

03

Resolution

Receive official updates and a final resolution directly on your registered email.
